SHADOW GASP — Hook Motion Prompts (Day 4 to Day 30)

Day 04: The Boy in the Box: Named, Not Solved
The young man's body flinches back sharply as his eyes snap wide and his mouth drops open in a silent gasp, camera holding tight on his shocked face and the box below him.

Day 05: Eilean Mor: The Storm That Never Was
The keeper freezes mid-stride as the door swings open, his eyes snapping wide and his mouth dropping open in a shocked gasp as he registers the overturned chair before him.

Day 06: The Amber Room: Six Tons of Gold That Vanished
The soldier's arm yanks downward with sudden force, the amber panel cracking free from the wall in a burst of golden light and dust, his mouth snapping open in a shout as he staggers back with the weight of it.

Day 07: Max Headroom Broadcast Intrusion
The masked face rocks side to side with a sudden jolt as static tears violently across the screen and the corrugated backdrop spins faster, the image glitching and stuttering as if the signal is fighting to hold.

Day 08: The Circleville Letters

Day 09: The Watcher - 657 Boulevard

Day 10: The Ship That Wouldn't Sink

Day 11: Chicago Tylenol Murders - The Killer Who Was Never Found

Day 12: Springfield Three - The Shattered Porch Light

Day 13: Yuba County Five - The Watch That Stopped

Day 14: Lufthansa: The Heist Nobody Paid For

Day 15: Who Put Bella in the Wych Elm

Day 16: The Axeman of New Orleans

Day 17: Brink's - The Vault That Never Gave It Back

Day 18: Ourang Medan - The Ship That Died Screaming

Day 19: The Five-Pound Note

Day 20: Baker Street Vault - The Tunnel Job

Day 21: The Banknotes That Weren't Fake

Day 22: Overtoun Bridge - The Dog Suicide Bridge

Day 23: The White House Boys

Day 24: The Book That Reads No One

Day 25: Mary Celeste: The Ship That Sailed Itself

Day 26: SS Waratah - The Ship That Vanished Without a Trace

Day 28: The PM Who Vanished Into the Surf

Day 29: The Antikythera Mechanism - Corpses, Corrosion, and a Machine Time Forgot

Day 30: The Ledger of Cerro Gordo
