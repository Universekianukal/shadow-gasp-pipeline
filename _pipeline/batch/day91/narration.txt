CCTV captures a 28-year-old man sprinting from Varna Airport. He drops his luggage mid-stride. He vaults a 9-foot fence. He vanishes into a wheat field. And is never seen again. July 8, 2014. The man was Lars Mittank, a German tourist vacationing at Golden Sands, Bulgaria. Three days earlier, a fight outside a club left him with a ruptured eardrum. A doctor prescribed Cefuroxime 500 milligrams, told him not to fly, and sent him to a hotel alone while his friends flew home. That night, Lars called his mother in Germany, whispering. Someone was trying to kill him. He begged her to cancel his credit cards. She assumed panic attack. On July 8, Lars arrived at Varna Airport. The airport doctor cleared him to fly. He walked toward the terminal. Then — on camera — something snapped. He burst out at full sprint, abandoned his bags, scaled the fence, and vanished into the fields. No phone, no wallet, no passport ever recovered. Bulgarian police searched the farmland for weeks. Nothing. His mother distributed thousands of flyers across Europe. Nothing. Lars Mittank has been missing for over 10 years. The footage has been viewed millions of times. Nobody knows what he was running from — or whether he is still running.
