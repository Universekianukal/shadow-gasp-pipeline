Three bodies in Cabin 28, bound with medical tape and electrical cord, beaten beyond recognition. April 11, 1981. Keddie, California — a former railroad resort shrunk to 60 residents in the Sierra Nevada foothills. Sue Sharp, 36. Her son John, 15. His friend Dana Wingate, 17. All three bludgeoned with a claw hammer and stabbed with a kitchen knife taken from the cabin's own kitchen. A fourth victim — Tina Sharp, 12 — vanished from the blood-soaked room. No body. No trail. The murder weapon was found in a pond behind a neighbor's house. That neighbor, Martin Smartt, had argued with Sue Sharp days before. His wife Marilyn told police Martin confessed to her — and described details only the killer could know. The lead sheriff failed to secure the cabin. Evidence walked out the door. Three years passed. In 1984, a bottle collector found Tina Sharp's skull 50 miles south near Feather Falls. No cause of death determined. Smartt died in 2000. Boubede in 1988. Neither was ever charged. Cabin 28 was demolished in 2004. The medical tape was never traced to a buyer.
