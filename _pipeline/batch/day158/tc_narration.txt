John Leary’s final radio call to the Coast Guard was calm. Position report, 180 miles off Jacksonville. "All is well." That was 9:13 AM. By 10 AM, the signal from his yacht’s satellite phone stops dead. At 11 AM, an Air Force crew spots the 47-foot M/Y Lively from the air. Drifting. Empty. The largest Coast Guard search since WWII scrambles 75,000 square miles of the Gulf Stream. They find one piece of debris. A white cooler lid. The twist? The life raft is still securely strapped to the deck, untouched. The emergency beacon was never activated. The Coast Guard’s final report is a void: "Presumed lost. No cause determined." A seaworthy boat, an experienced captain, calm seas… and five people simply erased. To this day, the Gulf Stream keeps its secret.
