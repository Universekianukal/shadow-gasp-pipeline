July 12, 1987. A man in a tailored suit walks into the Knightsbridge Security Deposit. He has a key. Rents safety deposit box 201. Hours later, he lets in four accomplices. They spend 12 hours inside the vault. 60 million pounds in jewels, cash, bonds. Gone. Police find the getaway car abandoned. A parking ticket leads to a rental agency. But the mastermind, Valerio Viccei, slips away to Italy. Months pass. Then, after robbing a bank in Rome, Viccei sends a postcard to Scotland Yard—bragging. He's arrested, extradited. But here's the twist: over half the loot is still missing. And 40% of box renters never came forward. What was in those unclaimed boxes? No one knows. The Knightsbridge heist remains a ghost story in ledgers.
